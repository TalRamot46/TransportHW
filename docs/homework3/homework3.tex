\section{The three solutions being compared}

Throughout, $\Sigma_t = v = 1$ as specified in the assignment, the source is the plane pulse
$Q(x,t) = \delta(x)\delta(t)$, and the figures are generated by \texttt{src/homework3}.

The \textbf{exact} solution is the planar-source scalar flux derived in Question 2 from the
Paasschens point-source solution, carried to general $c$ by the scaling identity of
Question 1:
\begin{equation}
    \phi(x,t;1) = \frac{e^{-t}}{2t}\left[1 + \sqrt{\frac{3}{\pi}}\int_0^{w_0}\sqrt{w}\,G(w)\,dw\right]\Theta(t-|x|),
    \qquad w_0 = t\left(1-\frac{x^2}{t^2}\right)^{3/4}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \phi(x,t;c) = c\,e^{-(1-c)t}\,\phi(cx,ct;1) \label{eq:scaling}
\end{equation}

The two \textbf{diffusion} solutions are the Green's functions of
\begin{equation}
    \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} - D\frac{\partial^2 \phi}{\partial x^2} + (1-c)\Sigma_t\phi = \delta(x)\delta(t)
    \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
    \phi_{\text{diff}}(x,t;c) = \frac{e^{-(1-c)t}}{\sqrt{4\pi D t}}\,e^{-x^2/(4Dt)} \label{eq:diffusion}
\end{equation}
differing only through $D = D_0(c)/\Sigma_t$:
\begin{equation}
    D_0 = \tfrac13 \quad\text{(classical)},
    \qquad
    D_0(c) = (1-c)\nu_0^2 \quad\text{(asymptotic)},
\end{equation}
where $\nu_0$ is the discrete transport eigenvalue, $c\nu_0\,\text{arctanh}(1/\nu_0) = 1$. For
$c>1$ the eigenvalue is imaginary, $\nu_0 = i|\nu_0|$, and $(1-c)\nu_0^2 = (c-1)|\nu_0|^2$
remains positive; both branches tend to $\tfrac13$ as $c\to1$, so the two approximations
coincide there. Integrating \eqref{eq:diffusion} over all $t$ recovers the familiar steady
result $\phi(x) = \frac{\sqrt3}{2\sqrt{1-c}}e^{-\sqrt{3(1-c)}\Sigma_t|x|}$, which fixes the
normalisation of \eqref{eq:diffusion}.
